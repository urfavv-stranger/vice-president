# managenius — Team Member Portfolio (White & Golden Edition)

An ultra-modern, high-end editorial portfolio crafted specifically for **Lochan Chugh (President, managenius)**. Inspired by the high-impact aesthetic of [Aryan's Portfolio](https://urfavv-stranger.github.io/ARYAN-PORTFOLIO/), customized with a **clean white & light theme**, **luxury 24K golden accents**, **dedicated individual about section**, **interactive dynamic cursor name color shifting**, and **prominent LinkedIn & Instagram connections**.

---

## 🌟 Key Layout & Structure

1. **Hero Section (Side-by-Side Desktop & Laptop Layout)**:
   - **Left Side**: President status badge, dynamic color-shifting name (`LOCHAN CHUGH`), executive bio, action buttons (`ABOUT MY ROLE`, `GET IN TOUCH`).
   - **Right Side**: Member portrait photo showcased inside an imperial 24K gold frame with rotating astrolabe radar rings and a gold `managenius PRESIDENT` badge.
   - **Bottom**: Infinite marquee ticker with gold celestial icons.

2. **Section 01 — About Myself (`#about-me`)**:
   - Clean 2-column executive profile: personal vision & milestones on the left, leadership responsibilities in managenius on the right.

3. **Section 02 — Connect & Socials (`#connect`)**:
   - High-impact glassmorphic link cards for **LinkedIn** and **Instagram** with golden border hover lift and direct link arrows.
   - Direct contact bar (Email, Society Affiliation, and Collaboration Status).

4. **Interactive Cursor Dynamic Name Color Shifting**:
   - Hovering or gliding your cursor across the name dynamically shifts between radiant liquid gold, champagne shimmer, sunburst amber, and gilded crown spectrums!
